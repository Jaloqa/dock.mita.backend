# Group Accounting 4b: To Confirm and Personal Records Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Group activity that touches a linked user's money reaches their accounts, budgets and reports only through a "To confirm" list. An expense the user paid, a repayment they made and a repayment they received appear there, also for past entries after a late link and again when a confirmed entry is edited or deleted. Confirming books it into an account of the user's choice in any currency: the user's share becomes an expense transaction in a category they choose, the rest of the money is an account movement that is neither income nor expense ("owed to me"), a payment back makes the shares it covers expenses on its date, and money received only enters the account. Rejecting books nothing. Repayments record which shares they cover: "Settle up" pays chosen shares, any other repayment covers the oldest open shares first.

**Scope:** Plan 4b of Delivery item 4; plan 4a (`2026-10-23-group-accounting-4a.md`, merged) built linking, invitations, linked members' access, standard category keys and group balances on the Debts page. Group balances, the Debts page figures and the share link do not change here.

**Architecture:**
- **Domain:** `GroupRepaymentCover` rows owned by `GroupRepayment` and the pure `GroupCoverAllocation` (requested shares first, then oldest first). Personal records: `GroupConfirmation` (one row per user and entry: the terms last decided, the terms the records reflect, the account used), `GroupConfirmationTransaction` (each transaction a confirmation wrote, with the expense its line is for) and `GroupAccountLeg` (a signed amount on an account, neither income nor expense).
- **Persistence:** migration `GroupRepaymentCovers` (table plus a SQL backfill of existing live repayments by the oldest-first rule) and migration `GroupConfirmations` (three tables; the `account_movements` view gains the legs). Personal records reference no group row, so they survive unlinking and group deletion.
- **API:** the repayment input gains `settles` and the repayment `covers`; `GET /v1/groups/{id}/open-shares`; a `ToConfirm` feature: `GET /v1/to-confirm`, `POST /v1/to-confirm/{kind}/{entryId}/confirm`, `POST /v1/to-confirm/{kind}/{entryId}/reject`. `Common/Groups/GroupTerms` computes what an entry means for a member, `Common/Groups/ToConfirmItems` the pending items, `Common/Groups/GroupRecordWriter` prices and writes transactions and legs.
- **Frontend:** repayment rows show what they cover; "Settle up" on the group page; a "To confirm" section on the Groups page with a confirm dialog (account, rate, one line per share with category, title and tags); a count on the Tools hub's Groups card. The mock backend implements the same rules.

**Tech Stack:** Backend: .NET 10, EF Core migrations (Npgsql, `jsonb`), MediatR 12.5.0, FluentValidation, xUnit v3, Shouldly, Testcontainers. Frontend: Nuxt 4, PrimeVue 4.5, TanStack Query, Vuelidate, bun:test, Playwright 1.64.1. No new packages in either repo.

**Spec:** `dock.mita.frontend/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-tools-design.md`: "Personal accounting for members with an account (option B)" in full, calls "Late linking", "Unlinking or deleting", "Standard category keys", Anti-goals "Inflated spending" and "Lost entries", Delivery item 4. Also binding: both repos' project guides and `docs/STACK.md`, backend `docs/api-contract.md`, the Decisions of plans 3a, 3b and 4a. Code to reuse: backend `Common/Groups/*` (`GroupAccess`, `GroupRepaymentWriter`, `GroupEntryEvents`, `GroupExpenseState`, `GroupRepaymentState`, `GroupRepaymentQueries`), `Common/Transactions/{TransactionPrice,TransactionReferenceRules,TransactionQueries}.cs`, `Common/Tags/{TagNames,TagWriter}.cs`, `Common/Rates/*`, `Common/Categories/*`, `Domain/Groups/MinorAllocation.cs`, `Domain/Categories/*`; frontend `app/features/groups/*`, `app/shared/{accounts,categories,rates,money,api}/*`, `app/features/debts/components/RepaymentDialog.vue` and `app/features/transactions/components/TransactionDialog.vue` (account and rate patterns only, not imported), `mocks/{db,groups,transactions,ledger,categories}.ts`.

| Topic | Decision | Cost if wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Plan split | 4b (this plan, both repos, own PRs, branch `feature/group-confirmations`) after 4a | None |
| What lands in "To confirm" | For each group where the user is linked now, with their member m: every expense whose payer is m and every repayment from or to m, deleted ones included, plus every entry the user already decided on. An expense someone else paid never lands there: the user's share stays "I owe" (group figures) until they pay it back | None |
| Terms | What an entry means for the user. Expense paid by m: `amount`, `currency`, `date`, `share` (m's share amount in the expense currency, 0 when m is not in the split), `categoryId`, `tags`, `description`. Repayment: `direction` (`paid` from m, `received` to m), `memberId` (the other member), `amount`, `currency`, `date`, `covers` (paid only). A deleted entry, or one that no longer involves m, has no terms (null). Terms are compared by their canonical JSON (serialized from the record) | Rearranging the other members' shares of an expense the user paid asks nothing (their share and the total are unchanged) |
| When an item is pending | Stored per user and entry: `HandledTerms` (the last decision) and `ConfirmedTerms` (what the records reflect; null = no records); no row = both null. Pending when the current terms differ from both. `change`: `new` without records, `removed` when the current terms are null, otherwise `changed`; `previous` = `ConfirmedTerms` | An entry changed and changed back before the user looked asks nothing (the records already match) |
| Late linking and leaving | Computed at read time: a member linked today brings every past entry it paid, sent or received. Leaving, being unlinked, a removed member or a deleted group hide the group's items; records already written stay as ordinary records (no FK to groups, members or entries) | None |
| Personal records | `group_confirmations` (`Id`, `UserId` FK users cascade, `GroupId`, `Kind`, `EntryId` without FK, `HandledTerms` and `ConfirmedTerms` `jsonb` null, `AccountId` FK accounts `SET NULL`, `DecidedAt`; unique `(UserId, Kind, EntryId)`); `group_confirmation_transactions` (`TransactionId` primary key, FK transactions cascade; `ConfirmationId` FK cascade; `ExpenseId` without FK); `group_account_legs` (`Id`, `UserId` FK users cascade, `ConfirmationId` FK cascade, `AccountId` FK accounts `NoAction`, `Date`, `AccountAmount` signed, check `<> 0`). The `account_movements` view gains `SELECT "UserId", "AccountId", "AccountAmount" FROM group_account_legs`, so legs count in `Account.balance`, `inUse`, `currencyLocked` and `accountInUse`. Booked transactions are ordinary transactions | None |
| Booking an expense the user paid | `A` = `amount` converted into the account currency as a transaction is (rate 1, the manual `rate` rounded by the rate rule, or the stored rate of the expense `date` under the 30-day rule: 503). `MinorAllocation.Allocate(A, [share, amount - share], [0, 1])`: the first part is an expense transaction (`Amount` = share in the expense currency, `AccountAmount` = the part, `Rate` = the rate used, `BaseAmount` as `TransactionPrice` prices the account amount into the base currency, `Date` = the expense date, category, title and tags from the line); the second part is a leg of minus that part on the expense date. A part of 0 is not written; `A` of 0 answers `rate: ["validation.positiveAmount"]`, above 100000000000 `rate: ["validation.invalidValue"]` | A share smaller than one minor unit of the account currency is not booked |
| Booking a repayment the user made | `A` = `amount` converted the same way at the repayment date. Weights: the `groupAmount` of each cover (cover order), then the uncovered rest (`groupAmount` minus the covers) when above 0. `amount` and `A` are each split with `MinorAllocation` by those weights; each cover becomes an expense transaction on the repayment date (`Amount` = its part of `amount` in the repayment currency, `AccountAmount` = its part of `A`), the uncovered part a leg of minus its part of `A`. Parts of 0 are not written | A cover too small for the rounding books nothing |
| Booking a repayment received | One leg of plus `A` on the repayment date | None |
| Changes and removals | Confirming a `changed` item deletes the transactions (through `group_confirmation_transactions`) and legs of the earlier confirmation and writes new ones; a `removed` item only deletes them. Rejecting sets `HandledTerms` and writes or removes nothing. A transaction the user deleted by hand is gone (its link row with it) | Edits the user made to a booked transaction are replaced when they confirm a later change of its entry |
| Lines | An item lists one line per transaction it would write: the expense paid (share above 0) or each cover of a repayment made. A line names `expenseId`, the expense `description`, `amount` and `currency` (its part of the entry, in the entry currency) and a `suggestion` | None |
| Suggestions | Account: the account of the earlier confirmation of the entry, else `null`. Per line: when a transaction of the earlier confirmation for the same expense still exists, its category, title and tag names; otherwise the category is the user's expense category whose `StandardKey` is the expense's standard key, the user's expense "Other" when that category was deleted, and for a group category the user's expense category with that name compared case-insensitively, else `newCategory` = the name; title = the expense `description`, else the group name; tags = the expense tags | A starter category whose kind was changed keeps its key under the other kind, so its suggestion falls back to Other |
| Confirm input | `{ version, accountId, rate?, lines: [{ categoryId?, newCategory?, title?, tags? }] }`, `lines` in item order and exactly as many as the item has. A line has exactly one of `categoryId` (an expense category of the user, not `transfer`) and `newCategory` (1-40 characters after trimming, no control characters; an expense category of the user with that name, compared case-insensitively, is used, otherwise one is created with color `chart-4`). `title` and `tags` follow the transaction rules. A `removed` item takes `{ version }`; other fields are ignored | None |
| Order of checks | shape 400 (`version`, `accountId`, `rate`, `lines`; line fields under `lines.<index>.categoryId`, `.newCategory`, `.title`, `.tags`; a wrong count under `lines`) → item 404 (not in the user's list, or not pending) → version 409 `group.itemChanged` → references 400 (`accountId`: `unknownAccount`/`archivedAccount`; `lines.<index>.categoryId`: `unknownCategory`) → 503 `rates.unavailable` → converted amount 400 (`rate`). Reject: shape 400 (`version`) → 404 → 409 | None |
| Concurrency | Confirm and reject open the group as the linked user under `FOR SHARE` (entry writes wait), recompute the item and compare `version` (the id of the entry's newest event), then lock the confirmation row (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`, or insert it; a unique-index race answers 409 `group.itemChanged`) | None |
| Covers | `group_repayment_covers` (`RepaymentId` FK cascade, `ExpenseId` FK `NoAction`, `GroupId` FK groups cascade (3a R4 precedent), `GroupAmount` > 0; key `(RepaymentId, ExpenseId)`), written with the repayment on create and edit. The open amount of a share is its `groupAmount` minus the covers of the other live repayments from the same member on that expense, never below 0. The shares named in `settles` are covered first (in expense order), then the oldest open shares of the pair (live expenses paid by `toMemberId` in which `fromMemberId` shares, by `date`, creation time, `id`); what is left stays uncovered. Repayment create and edit lock the group `FOR UPDATE` so two repayments never cover the same open amount. Delete, restore and expense edits never change covers | Covers do not follow later edits of the expenses they cover: a share edited down after it was paid stays covered at the old amount |
| `settles` | Optional array of up to 50 distinct expense ids (not an array, more than 50, a repeat or a non-GUID: `settles: ["validation.invalidValue"]`); each must be a live expense of the group paid by `toMemberId` in which `fromMemberId` has a share (`settles: ["validation.unknownShare"]`, reported with the member references). `GroupRepayment`, `GroupRepaymentState` and history gain `covers: [{ expenseId, groupAmount }]` (state of older events: `[]`) | None |
| Existing repayments | The `GroupRepaymentCovers` migration backfills covers of every live repayment by the oldest-first rule in one SQL statement: per (from, to) pair, running sums of the debtor's live shares (expense order) and of the live repayments (repayment order: `date`, creation time, `id`); a cover is the overlap of the two intervals when above 0 | None |
| Open shares | `GET /v1/groups/{id}/open-shares` (user scope only, not on the link): the caller's member's shares with an open amount above 0, oldest first: `{ expenseId, payerId, date, description, categoryId, share, open }`, amounts in the main currency; an unlinked owner gets `[]`. Not paginated (bounded by the group's expenses, like members) | None |
| Settle up | Group page action for a linked user with open shares: shares grouped by payer with checkboxes, "Mark as paid" writes one repayment per payer in the main currency dated today, `amount` = the selected open amounts, `settles` = the selected expenses. The repayments then appear in "To confirm" | A user who paid in another currency records it with "Record repayment" |
| Wire | `GroupTerms = { amount, currency, date, share?, categoryId?, tags?, description?, direction?, memberId?, covers? }` (expense fields for expenses, repayment fields for repayments, the others omitted). `ToConfirmItem = { kind: 'expense' \| 'repayment', entryId, groupId, groupName, groupCurrency, change: 'new' \| 'changed' \| 'removed', version, at, actor: GroupActor, current: GroupTerms \| null, previous: GroupTerms \| null, other, lines: ToConfirmLine[], suggestedAccountId }` (`at`/`actor` of the newest event; `other` = the part that becomes a leg, in the entry currency: owed to the user, uncovered, or received; 0 for removed); `ToConfirmLine = { expenseId, description, amount, currency, suggestion: { categoryId, newCategory, title, tags } }`. `GET /v1/to-confirm?page&pageSize` (newest `at` first, then `entryId` descending; `pageSize` default and maximum 100); confirm and reject answer 204; the path `kind` is `expenses` or `repayments` (anything else 404) | None |
| Group data never changes | Confirm and reject never write a group table; link routes reach none of this (user policy) | None (anti-goal "Leaked groups") |
| Tools hub | The Groups card shows "{count} to confirm" (plural forms) when the list is not empty (`GET /v1/to-confirm?pageSize=1`, `total`) | None |
| Mock data | Entries unchanged; the seed backfills covers (Kasia → Anna 20 € covers the Dinner share 2000); mock categories carry `standardKey`; `MockState.version` 7 with `groupRepaymentCovers`, `groupConfirmations`, `groupConfirmationTransactions`, `groupLegs`; the mock ledger counts legs | Developer mock data resets once |

## Review Focus

- Paying for others must never inflate spending: after confirming a 120.00 € expense with a 30.00 € share, the account drops by the whole amount, the overview expense, the budget's spent amount and the report structure grow by the share only, and income stays 0. Pinned in Task 3 (`Should_BookOnlyTheShare_AsSpending`).
- Money received back and the uncovered part of a payment must never be income or expense, and a payment back must turn only the covered shares into expenses, dated on the payment. Pinned in Task 4 (`Should_NeverCountRepayments_AsIncome`, `Should_BookCoveredShares_OnThePaymentDate`).
- Nothing personal changes without a confirmation: after an edit or delete of a confirmed entry the transactions and balances stay as they were until the user confirms the change, rejecting changes nothing, and unlinking or deleting the group keeps the records. Pinned in Task 3 (`Should_KeepRecords_UntilChangeConfirmed`, `Should_KeepRecords_When_GroupDeletedOrUnlinked`).
- A user must only ever see and decide their own items: entries of other members, of groups where they are not linked, and every route for anonymous or link callers answer 404/401 and write nothing. Pinned in Task 2 (`Should_ListOnlyOwnItems`) and Task 3 (`Should_Return404_ForItemsOfOthers`).
- A stale or doubled confirmation must never book twice: an outdated `version` answers 409 and writes nothing, and two simultaneous confirmations of one item book it once. Pinned in Task 3 (`Should_Return409_When_ItemChangedMeanwhile`, `Should_BookOnce_When_ConfirmedTwiceAtOnce`).
